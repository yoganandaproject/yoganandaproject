A research index of external sites that organize useful Yogananda-related material, even when their text and images should not be copied into this archive.

Copyright note: the absence of a legal notice does not place a website into the public domain. External sites should be treated as research leads unless they clearly grant reuse rights or the underlying items are independently verified as public domain.

Indexed external site: Yogananda Site

yoganandasite.wordpress.com is a large lay devotee site that appears to function as a curated topical index. It is especially useful for locating monastic stories, disciple tribute pages, teachings-oriented article clusters, and organization-history essays.

Indexed external site: Yogananda Harmony

yoganandaharmony.com is useful as a devotional and reminiscence-oriented lead source, especially for stories, quotations, personal essays, and pointers toward talks and historical figures connected to Yogananda’s influence.

High-value leads identified from the crawl

  • Direct-disciple roundup pages for Daya Mata, Mrinalini Mata, Brother Anandamoy, and Brother Bhaktananda.
  • Individual tribute or memorial posts for Rajarsi Janakananda, Durga Mata, Tara Mata, Sister Gyanamata, Ananda Mata, Richard Wright, Brother Premamoy, and others.
  • Teachings-related posts on the Bhagavad Gita, SRF/YSS Lessons, Yogananda’s writings, and later editorial history.
  • Organization-history posts on SRF incorporation, monastic order, Mother Center, and archival letters.
  • Yogananda Harmony leads for reminiscence essays, quotations, image leads, and later influence stories that may help expand the archive cautiously.

How it should be used

  • As a discovery aid for subjects, titles, and possible primary-source citations.
  • As a pointer toward official SRF/YSS pages, scans, or videos where those are linked or named.
  • Not as a blanket license to republish its text or images.
