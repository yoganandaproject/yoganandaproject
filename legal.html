Yogananda Project

Legal and project disclaimer

This website is a non-commercial, open-source historical archive project intended to document and organize public-domain and otherwise reusable material as carefully as possible.

Non-commercial purpose

The Yogananda Project is intended solely as a public-interest educational and historical resource. It is not operated as a commercial service, does not exist to sell products or subscriptions, and is not intended for monetization. The project’s stated purpose is to preserve, organize, and make easier to study public-domain and otherwise reusable historical material related to Paramahansa Yogananda, his works, his close circles, and related archival subjects.

The project is intended to remain non-commercial. It is maintained as an open-source GitHub-hosted website, and the working repository is part of the project’s public transparency.

Accuracy and historical limitations

This archive is assembled in good faith, but it may contain mistakes, incomplete records, disputed interpretations, date errors, attribution errors, or outdated research conclusions. Historical data, especially in spiritual, family, organizational, and reminiscence contexts, can be difficult to verify conclusively.

No warranty is made that any page is complete, error-free, or suitable for legal, scholarly, or genealogical reliance without further independent verification. Readers should consult primary sources, official publications, and additional documentation wherever accuracy is material.

Rights and reuse policy

The project attempts to include only material believed to be in the public domain or otherwise clearly reusable, and it aims to record provenance and rights notes wherever possible. However, rights determinations can be complex and jurisdiction-specific, and a mistaken inclusion does not mean the project claims ownership or unlimited redistribution rights.

The absence of a copyright notice on an external website does not mean that website’s text, images, or media are public domain. External sites may be indexed here as research leads without their content being republished.
