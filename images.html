Research-only external leads

yoganandaharmony.com contains stories, devotional essays, and image leads related to Yogananda and Swami Kriyananda, but it should currently be treated as a research source only unless a page explicitly states reusable licensing.

Noted leads include its Yogananda biography page, quotations from recordings, and personal reminiscence essays that may point toward names, events, and archival materials worth verifying elsewhere.

Research-only notable influence list

Current research points to several later public figures who were strongly influenced by Yogananda or Autobiography of a Yogi. This list is distinct from direct disciples and from important contemporaries like Luther Burbank and M. K. Gandhi.

  • George Harrison
  • Elvis Presley
