Yogananda Project

Original publications

A research inventory for original publications by Paramahansa Yogananda and early SRF publishing activity, with a focus on items that may be in the public domain.

Rights note: suspected public-domain status is not enough on its own. Each issue, edition, scan, and download source should be checked individually before the site treats it as a reusable hosted file.

Why this section exists

Early Yogananda and SRF publications are central to the project: magazines, pamphlets, books, and lecture materials often preserve original texts, contemporary advertisements, editorial framing, and historical images that later editions omit.

This page is designed to track promising PDF and scan leads first, then separate them into fully verified public-domain items, research-only references, and locally hosted derivatives.
