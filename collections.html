Yogananda Project

Collections to build next

Phase 1 defines the major archive areas before deeper data entry begins, including family and guru lineage research.

Original works

Books, lectures, lessons, pamphlets, poems, letters, and verified public-domain editions.

Future fields: title, date, format, publication history, archive location, rights status.

Direct disciples and related people

Disciples, family members, gurus, their families, their disciples, friends, colleagues, and later custodians whose records help map the historical network.

Future fields: normalized name, dates, affiliations, roles, works, citations, relationships.

Notable non-disciple figures influenced by Yogananda

Friends, associates, cultural transmitters, and later readers whose lives or work were strongly shaped by Yogananda or Autobiography of a Yogi.

Examples: Luther Burbank, Steve Jobs, George Harrison, Elvis Presley, Gary Wright, and other documented readers or associates.

Family and guru lineages

Connected records for Yogananda's family, guru-parampara relationships, and the family and disciple networks surrounding those teachers.

Future fields: lineage position, kinship, teacher-student links, geography, associated works, citations.

Images

Portraits, group photos, event images, scans, and publication illustrations with source and rights notes.

Future fields: subject, date, photographer if known, repository, provenance, usage status.

Audio and video

Recorded talks, interviews, documentaries, oral histories, and film footage that can be verified and cleared for use.

Future fields: runtime, format, archive location, speakers, transcript status, rights status.
