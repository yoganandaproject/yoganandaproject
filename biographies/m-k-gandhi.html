M. K. Gandhi

Contemporary, friend, and recipient of Kriya Yoga initiation from Yogananda.

Biography

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi belongs in this archive as a major non-disciple contemporary whose life intersected directly with Paramahansa Yogananda. Yogananda met Gandhi during his return visit to India in 1935–1936, and accounts associated with Yogananda's published work state that Gandhi received Kriya Yoga initiation from him.

Gandhi is therefore not merely a later admirer or indirect influence case. He represents an important historical bridge between Yogananda's spiritual mission and the moral, political, and religious life of twentieth-century India.
