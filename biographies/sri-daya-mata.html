Sri Daya Mata

Served as SRF/YSS President for 55 years.

Biography

Sri Daya Mata, born Rachel Faye Wright on January 31, 1914, in Salt Lake City, became one of Paramahansa Yogananda's early monastic disciples after meeting him in 1931. She entered the Mount Washington ashram the same year and later became known for her administrative skill, shorthand transcription work, and assistance in preparing Yogananda's teachings for publication.

During Yogananda's lifetime she took on increasing responsibility within Self-Realization Fellowship, including leadership at the Mount Washington headquarters and editorial work related to Autobiography of a Yogi. After the deaths of Yogananda and Rajarsi Janakananda, she was elected president of SRF/YSS in 1955.

She remained president until her death in 2010, making her one of the longest-serving women leaders of a global Hindu-based spiritual organization in the twentieth century.
