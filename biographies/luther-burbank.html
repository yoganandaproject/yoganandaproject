Luther Burbank

Botanist, friend of Yogananda, and dedicatee of Autobiography of a Yogi.

Biography

Luther Burbank was an American botanist and horticultural innovator whose work made him one of the best-known plant breeders of his era. He is significant in this archive not as a direct disciple in the usual SRF sense, but as an important friend and respected associate of Paramahansa Yogananda during Yogananda's American years.

Autobiography of a Yogi is dedicated to Burbank, and the book describes him as a renowned botanist whom Yogananda visited in America. That dedication makes him one of the clearest examples of a major non-disciple figure personally connected to Yogananda's life and mission.
