Swami Satyananda Giri

Headed Ranchi school and YSS work in India.

Biography

Swami Satyananda Giri, born Manamohan Mazumder on November 17, 1896, was a close childhood friend and brother-disciple of Paramahansa Yogananda. Raised in a family that later lived near the Ghosh household in Calcutta, he became part of the same Kriya Yoga milieu shaped by Sri Yukteswar and the Lahiri Mahasaya lineage.

He studied philosophy at the University of Calcutta and became a disciple of Swami Sri Yukteswar Giri. After Yogananda left for America in 1920, Satyananda played a major role in sustaining the Ranchi school, serving as principal and secretary for roughly two decades.

He later led institutions at Puri and Sevayatan and authored several Bengali works on Lahiri Mahasaya, Sri Yukteswar, and Yogananda.
