Swami Rajarsi Janakananda

Chief benefactor and spiritual successor.

Biography

Swami Rajarsi Janakananda, born James Jesse Lynn on May 5, 1892, in Archibald, Louisiana, became one of Paramahansa Yogananda's closest American disciples after meeting him in Kansas City in 1932. Before entering Yogananda's circle, Lynn had built a major business career in accounting, insurance, oil, and rail investments.

After taking initiation in Kriya Yoga, Lynn became a principal benefactor of Self-Realization Fellowship and helped secure the long-term institutional future of Yogananda's work. He funded Yogananda's 1935 return trip to India and purchased the Encinitas property that became one of SRF's most important centers.

In 1951 Yogananda gave him monastic vows and the name Rajarsi Janakananda, and after Yogananda's death in 1952 he served as the second president of SRF/YSS until his own death in 1955.
