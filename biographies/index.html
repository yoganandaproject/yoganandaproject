Important non-disciple figures and later admirers

This archive also tracks people who were not direct disciples but were either personally connected to Yogananda or were later deeply influenced by his teachings and Autobiography of a Yogi.

  • Luther Burbank — friend, associate, and dedicatee of Autobiography of a Yogi.
  • M. K. Gandhi — contemporary, friend, and recipient of Kriya Yoga initiation from Yogananda.
  • Research-only influence list: Steve Jobs, George Harrison, Elvis Presley, Gary Wright, Mariel Hemingway, Dennis Weaver, Linda Evans, Andrew Weil, Jon Anderson, and Rafael Reyes.
