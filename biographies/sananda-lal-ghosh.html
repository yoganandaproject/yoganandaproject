Sananda Lal Ghosh

Younger brother and author of Mejda.

Biography

Sananda Lal Ghosh was one of Paramahansa Yogananda's younger brothers and an important family source for Yogananda's early life. His recollections helped preserve the domestic, devotional, and social world of the Ghosh family from which Mukunda Lal Ghosh emerged.

He is best known in English-speaking Yogananda circles as the author of Mejda: The Family and the Early Life of Paramahansa Yogananda, a family-centered memoir that has often been used by later biographers and students of Yogananda's life.
