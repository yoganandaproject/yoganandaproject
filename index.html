Yogananda Project

Building an open historical archive of Yogananda's works, lineages, disciples, and media.

This site is being built as a static, research-focused resource dedicated to Paramhansa Yogananda's public-domain legacy: original writings, images, audio, video, and the family, guru, and disciple networks closest to his life and work.

Direct disciples Collections Methodology

Phase 1 scope

The first phase creates a stable public starting point: a homepage, a seed directory of direct disciples, initial family and guru-lineage coverage, collection definitions, and research standards for future data entry.

Initial priorities

  • Document original works and public-domain editions.
  • Catalog direct disciples, family members, gurus, and related lineages.
  • Track photographs, audio, video, and archival references.
  • Define verification and provenance rules before expansion.

Core collections

Works

Bibliographic records for Yogananda's writings, lectures, pamphlets, and related public-domain publications across his broader lineage network.

People

A growing directory of direct disciples, family members, gurus, their families, their disciples, friends, colleagues, and their own works where historically relevant.

Media

Structured inventories of verified images, audio, and video with source notes and rights status.
